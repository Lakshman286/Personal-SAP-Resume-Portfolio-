ZAGRI_FARMER
Master Data
Purpose: Customer / Farmer master
Stores farmer profile, verified phone, regional farming cluster, landholding acreage, and seasonal cultivation history. Primary key: FARMER_ID.
ZAGRI_CROP
Master Data
Purpose: Crop master
Master catalog of regional crops, seasonal suitability (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid), typical lifecycle stages, and optimal soil profiles. Primary key: CROP_ID.
ZAGRI_PESTICIDE
Shop Inventory
Purpose: Pesticide shop inventory and catalog
Chemical formulations, active ingredients, package units, retail pricing, and real-time shop stock levels. Primary key: PEST_ID.
ZAGRI_PEST_CROP
Relational Mapping
Purpose: Pesticide–crop applicability mapping
Relational applicability matrix verifying which chemical treatments are legally and biologically authorized for specific crops. Composite key: (PEST_ID, CROP_ID).
ZAGRI_CROP_PEST
Advisory Engine
Purpose: Crop and pest advisory matrix (primary and alternative pesticide)
Consulted by the recommendation engine: pairs crop pests with primary pesticide regimens and verified substitute chemicals during stockouts. Composite key: (CROP_ID, PEST_ID).
ZAGRI_RECOMMEND
Audit Log
Purpose: Advisory session log
Historical advisory log preserving session transactions, identified crop pests, recommended formulations, chemical dosages, and sy-datum timestamps. Primary key: REC_ID.
ZAGRI_FERTILIZER
Shop Inventory
Purpose: Fertilizer master and inventory
Nutrient composition (N-P-K), unit bag weights, standard pricing, warehouse inventory counts, and automated reorder alerts. Primary key: FERT_ID.
ZAGRI_FERT_CROP
Relational Mapping
Purpose: Fertilizer–crop mapping
Nutrient dosage guidelines per acre, application timing (basal vs top dressing), and soil compatibility formulas. Composite key: (FERT_ID, CROP_ID).
ZAGRI_SALES_HDR
Sales Transaction
Purpose: Sales billing header
Billing invoice header recording billing document number, farmer reference, billing date, net gross amount, tax routines, and SmartForms PDF status. Primary key: SALES_ID.
ZAGRI_SALES_ITEM
Stock Deduction
Purpose: Pesticide sales line items and inventory deduction
Pesticide billing line items; upon sales order confirmation, triggers automated stock deduction logic against ZAGRI_PESTICIDE. Composite key: (SALES_ID, POSNR).
ZAGRI_FERT_SALES_ITEM
Stock Deduction
Purpose: Fertilizer sales line items and inventory deduction
Fertilizer sales items; executes real-time inventory updates and quantity deduction against ZAGRI_FERTILIZER. Composite key: (SALES_ID, POSNR).
